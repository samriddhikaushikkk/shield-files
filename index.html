ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

THE INTERNATIONAL ANOMALY & RECORDS DIVISION

The International Anomaly and Records Division, abbreviated as IARD, is a fictional organisation established to document unexplained incidents, unusual discoveries and mysterious historical records.

Its archive is divided into multiple departments, each responsible for collecting, reviewing and preserving unusual information.
